# App Factory

The working brain for a one-person app business (find ideas, design apps with their own identity, keep users, earn money, pass the stores) and the apps themselves, each in its own folder under `apps/`.

**First app in progress:** `apps/paper-puzzles/` (working title Paper Pines), a calm daily logic-puzzle game.

- **Anass:** open `START_HERE.md`.
- **Claude:** `CLAUDE.md` loads by itself. Open other files only when the task needs them.

```
CLAUDE.md        how to work with Anass (read every session)
CHEATSHEET.md    the 15 steps from "make me an app" to the stores
START_HERE.md    Anass's guide
ROADMAP.md       the plan and the gates
IDEAS.md         20 apps to choose from, scored
briefs/          one full brief per app idea (01 to 20)
apps/            the real apps, one folder each
WORLDS.md        the looks we already used (never repeat one)
PROMPTS.md       copy-paste prompts
PROGRESS.md      done / next / blocked
DECISIONS.md     what changed and why
playbook/        00 Anass · 01 method · 02 ideas · 03 open source · 04 anti-slop · 05 worlds
                 06 retention · 07 money · 08 stores · 09 countries · 10 security
                 11 launch · 12 tokens and agents · 13 Gobblo lessons
research/        dated facts with sources
tools/           roi.py · dda_sim.py · contrast.py   (python3, no installs)
templates/       app-starter/ · idea-card.md · worlds.json
.claude/         settings, agents, skills
```

Facts were checked on 13 Nov 2026. Store rules and payment rules change: re-check `research/` before any money decision or store submission. Nothing here is legal, tax or financial advice, and nothing here promises income.
