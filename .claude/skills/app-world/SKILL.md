# /app-world
1. Read `playbook/05-app-worlds.md`, `playbook/04-design-anti-slop.md` (the method), `WORLDS.md`, the app's `docs/brief.md` and its review research.
2. Write the person and the moment from real evidence. Pick three feeling words that are wrong for every app in `WORLDS.md`.
3. Collect references: 6 apps of the category and 6+ things from the user's real life. Save them with one line each in the app's `research/refs/`.
4. Write 3 directions (a paragraph each: material, colour, type, motion, sound, voice, signature). They must differ from each other and from `WORLDS.md`.
5. Make each one visible: one key screen per direction (an image concept, or a quick real screen). Send the three to Anass and ask him to pick or mix. Stop here until he answers.
6. After his pick: write `docs/world.md`, build the single token file, run `python3 tools/contrast.py <palette file>`, add the row to `WORLDS.md` in this repo, log the decision.
