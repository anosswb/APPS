# Free app stores and the domain question (15 Nov 2026)

Asked by Anass: "Is there any store I can put it on for free, and can I start without buying a domain?"
Checked with web search on 15 Nov 2026. Labels: page = official page in the results; snippet = search snippet only.

| Store | Fee to publish | Catch | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Samsung Galaxy Store | No sign-up fee, no yearly fee (page) | To publish even free apps you need "commercial seller" status; forum posts say it means a registered business verified with a D-U-N-S number (snippet, older posts: re-check on the Seller Portal). Revenue share 80/20 since March 2025 (page). Only Samsung phones. | developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store/overview.html · /sdp/news/en/2025/03/13/new-revenue-share-model-for-galaxy-store |
| Amazon Appstore | — | Closed on Android phones on 20 Aug 2025; only Fire tablets and Fire TV now (several news sites) | 9to5google.com/2025/02/20/amazon-appstore-android-shutting-down-2025/ |
| Huawei AppGallery | Not confirmed | Not checked. Few US users (Huawei phones are not sold in the US) | — |
| Google Play | $25 once (our 13 Nov research; not re-checked today) | New personal accounts: closed test, 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row before going public (Google help page) | support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465 |
| Apple App Store | $99 a year (our 13 Nov research) | — | — |

**Domain:** not needed to start. The stores need a privacy policy page on the web, and a free page works (for example GitHub Pages from a small public repo, or Google Sites). A domain (about $15 a year) can come later.
