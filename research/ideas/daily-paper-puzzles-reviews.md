## 4. LOVE (do not break these)
- **Calm, low-stimulus play, "no nonsense".** "it’s very low stimuli and there’s nothing moving" ([Star Battle 10102273818](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1494377962?see-all=reviews)); "I love the puzzles-no time pressure, no ads, no tracking." ([Star Battle 11916797218](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1494377962?see-all=reviews)). Calm/relaxing is named in about 22 four-to-five-star reviews.
- **Non-punishing, instructive hints and a real tutorial.** "I love that the hint system in this game isn’t punishing." ([Tents iOS 9471161041](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1279378379?see-all=reviews)); "The hints are instructive!!" ([Tents iOS 10174186632](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1279378379?see-all=reviews)); a tutorial that "walks you thru every step" won over a skeptic ([Nonogram 14712684228](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1452992954?see-all=reviews)).
- **Works offline, short daily sessions, free daily puzzles.** "works without wifi" ([Nonogram 14719724496](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1452992954?see-all=reviews)); free dailies "take a total of maybe 15-20 minutes" ([Tents iOS 6346438656](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1279378379?see-all=reviews)).
- **Puzzles solvable by logic, a clear easy-to-hard ladder, a clean UI.** "solvable without having to resort to trial and error" ([Star Battle 7415012693](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1494377962?see-all=reviews)); "there are no pop-ups and the customization is thought out." ([Star Battle 8178978298](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1494377962?see-all=reviews)). ## 5. UNSOLVED (no app gets all of these right) - partly my inference
- A fair way to pay: the apps with a nice one-time pack (Star Battle) sell content that repeats and cannot be tracked; the app with the free model (Nonogram.com, Sudoku.com) is now ad-saturated; Tents makes players buy coins. None offers "pay once, get everything, no ads, no coins".
- Hints that teach the technique (named deduction, e.g. "this row is full, so these cells are grass") without a penalty. Players ask for it in 8 reviews; Tents hints are praised but are not described as explanatory.
- Calm AND varied: players who want calm also hate event/side-mode clutter, yet they want fresh daily content and an index of what they solved.
- Fair difficulty ladder with a medium step, plus a promise of logic-only solving (3 reviews doubt that logic alone is enough).
- Dark mode and large tap targets are still missing from the biggest app (Nonogram.com), 14 fans ask for it.

## 6. The gap and 5 rules
**Gap (one sentence):** Nobody offers a calm, ad-free, logic-only puzzle app that you pay for once, with hints that actually explain the deduction and nothing interrupting a move in progress.
1. **Never interrupt a move or a puzzle in progress.** No ads, pop-ups, "Great move" animations or event prompts mid-puzzle; this is the single biggest reason for 1-star reviews (~145 of ~170 low-star ad complaints in the two big apps). Android ads only between puzzles, never after each one, with a visible close button from the first frame.
2. **Sell one price that unlocks everything, with no coins.** About 15 players say they would pay once, and ~$15 for ad removal is called absurd. Show a clear price, "no ads" wording that is literally true, and keep daily puzzles free.
3. **Hints explain the logic and cost nothing.** Name the technique, highlight the cells, and split "check my mistakes" from "show a clue". No lives, no hearts, no game over, no wrong-answer pop-ups; offer an optional timer/stats screen, off by default.
4. **Build calm basics at launch:** dark mode, big tap targets and zoom, drag-to-fill, undo that undoes one step, works offline, a solved/unsolved puzzle index with no repeats. Dark mode alone was asked for 19 times.
5. **Protect progress and trust in every update:** cloud/device sync, test restore of saved games, never move or remove a setting or button location without a toggle, and avoid AI-looking art (3 complaints, with real-artist art praised).
