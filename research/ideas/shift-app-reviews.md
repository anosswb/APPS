# Shift app — what users say (14 Nov 2026)
Made by the review-miner helper.
**Read:** Shifter, App Store US: 274 reviews, 2022 to Nov 2026 (83% five-star, so few complaints) · Shift Calendar (skedsolutions), Google Play US: 200 reviews, 2020 to Mar 2024 (older pain) · Shift Master, App Store US: 72 reviews, mostly firefighters, mostly 2012–2018.
**NOT read:** Supershift, MyShiftPlanner, NurseGrid (the reading tool ran out of credits; back on 4 Dec 2026). **Reddit and the trade forums were blocked: zero real blue-collar threads were read.** The blue-collar section below is therefore a gap, not a finding.

## What they hate
1. **Sync and calendar links that break** (biggest theme). "I paid the price for this so I could share it with my wife on Google Calendar and it will not work." (Shifter, Feb 2026)
2. **Lost data, no move to a new phone.** "The backup/restore functions don't work, so you'll have to re-create all your shifts… any time you get a new phone." (Google Play, Feb 2023)
3. **Dates that drift.** "Keeps moving my days from one month to another one and messing up my schedule." (Shifter, Feb 2025)
4. **Hard setup of long rotations.** "Spent an hour trying to figure it out. Then gave up and uninstalled it." (Shift Master, 2018)
5. **Widgets that break.** "The widget is a month behind… until you open the actual app." · "WE NEED OUR WIDGET BACK!!" (Shifter, Sep 2024)
6. **Silent support after the sale.** "Won't sync to new phone and no response from developer!"
7. Nagging to rate, and subscriptions: only one or two reviews each. People complain about broken paid features more than about the price.

## What they love
Set the rotation once and see months ahead · colours per shift · quick bulk entry · total hours · a working widget · simple and cheap.

## What they ask for and do not get
- **Payday on the calendar and hours per pay period.** "It asks for a pay day but it doesn't recognise a pay day and it doesn't automatically calculate."
- More than two shifts on one day · import from another calendar · an alarm you can change · a mark for partial overtime.

## Blue-collar needs: NOT VERIFIED
- Reviews do show plant workers, firefighters and medics using these apps (a "chemical plant", "VW's auto plant", 24/48 and 48/96 rotations).
- **No review in the sample asked for overtime rules, shift differential, attendance points, time-off balance or a picture of the month.** Payday and hour totals did come up. The widget is both loved and a source of anger.
- How these workers track schedules today, forced overtime, checking the paycheck, sleep, telling the family: not read. Do not guess.
- To finish this: allow reddit.com and practicalmachinist.com in the session network settings, or have Anass paste 10–15 threads from r/AmazonFC, r/Truckers, r/Machinists.

## Rules for our app
1. Backup and restore are free, tested and on by default.
2. Never sell a sync we do not control. Export to the phone's calendar and say plainly what it does.
3. Dates are worked out from the rotation rule, never stored month by month.
4. Test every named rotation (2-2-3, 4 on 4 off, DuPont, Pitman, 3×12, 24/48, 48/96, 28-day) and show the next 12 months before saving.
5. The widget is core: it must never show the wrong month.
6. Payday and hours per pay period on the main screen.
7. Answer support and restore purchases fast.
8. Prove the points tracker and the overtime rules with real workers before building them.

## The gap
The existing apps show when you work, but they lose or break that data on sync, phone changes and updates, and none read here turns the schedule into pay-period hours with payday on screen. Whether blue-collar workers want overtime, differential or points in the same app is still unproven.
