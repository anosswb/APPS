## 3. Complaints
I could NOT open store reviews or Reddit. No verbatim quotes obtained. Below are paraphrases from pages seen (not quotes):

4. Praise to copy: "way more puzzles than other logic apps" (Twin Wizards) and hints that explain (brennerd listing). [report]
Not found: forced guessing, tiny cells, subscription anger for Tents apps. Treat these as untested guesses.

## 5. The gap
They all lean on ads, coins or subscriptions around the puzzle (and look like generic apps), and nobody sells a calm, no-timer, newspaper-styled Tents page with one shared daily puzzle and hints that explain the logic for a one-time price. (Partial exceptions: brennerd has explaining hints; LogiBrain and Tatham are free; Tents & Trails/Forest Camp have a daily puzzle and may not have the newspaper look: not verified.)
