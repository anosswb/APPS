## Blue-collar focus
- No consumer app built for blue-collar workers was found. The marketing is for nurses or "everyone". TeamShift names factory workers in its text only.
- **Attendance points:** no consumer app found. Only a free web template (points drop off after 90 days), enterprise HR tools, and trackers sold on Etsy for a few dollars.
- **Overtime and differential:** many web calculators; no shift calendar found that does overtime rules or shift differential. One vendor page (TimeTrex) says a differential must be blended into the regular rate before overtime is counted: with $8 and $9 hours the regular rate is $8.50, so overtime is $12.75, not $12. **Check this against the US Department of Labor's own page before building pay maths.**

## How many people
16% of US wage and salary workers usually worked a non-daytime schedule in 2017–18, about 6 million of them nights (US Bureau of Labor Statistics release of 24 Sep 2019, via search). No newer count found.
