# Dock load — what players say (14 Nov 2026)
Made by the review-miner helper. **Read:** Block Blast! iPhone US (196 reviews, 11–13 Nov) and Google Play US (193, 12–13 Nov) · Woodoku iPhone (120, Jul–Nov) · Blockudoku iPhone (150, Aug–Nov) · Fill The Fridge! iPhone (238, Jun–Nov) · 12 Reddit posts in r/blockblast.
**Limits:** the Block Blast sample comes right after a viral "Block Blast is rigged" video (13 Oct), so that theme is probably inflated. Whether any game really rigs its pieces is NOT verified; these are players' claims. Churn cannot be measured from reviews.

## What they hate
1. **Too many ads** (biggest theme in all five). "After every loss… in 5 minutes you probably have 20 ads." (Block Blast) · "Horribly frequent unstoppable ads ruin any relaxation." (Woodoku)
2. **Ads in the middle of a move.** "An ad popping up WHILE moving a piece, resulting in that piece played in the wrong spot." · "The first time I got an ad mid puzzle… they lost me, I'm out, deleting the app." (Woodoku)
3. **An ad even after saying no.** "Oh you want an ad to revive? ...No? OK here's an ad." (Block Blast)
4. **Ads hard to close, scam ads, fake gameplay in ads.** "It takes you 6-8 times of pressing the X." (Woodoku) · "Nothing like the adds" (about 30 Blockudoku reviews)
5. **"Remove ads" missing or broken.** "Paid for no ads and still getting them." · "I'd be happy to pay for an ad-free version, but there's no option." (Woodoku)
6. **Needs internet.** "I downloaded this game before my flight… can't play this game at all." (Fill The Fridge)
7. **Updates that remove the calm.** "Wasn't trying to spike your dopamine every 5 seconds." (Blockudoku)
8. **Paying to fix a slip of the finger.** (Fill The Fridge)

## Fairness of the pieces: they notice
The pattern players describe: easy pieces while far from their best score, impossible pieces when they get close to it, then an ad offers a revive.
- "The game will give you an unsolvable puzzle right when you are about to reach your high score. It makes you watch an ad." (Block Blast)
- "The game gives a 3x3 block even though there is no available 3x3 space on the board." (Block Blast)
- "Your skill doesn't matter anymore so why play." (Blockudoku)
- "I would prefer to just receive random pieces." (Blockudoku)
- "They will instantly give you blocks that don't fit the game board and you fail, which I think is unfair due to the fact that you have to watch an ad after." (Blockudoku)
- "An ai game bot continually gives you outright laughably large pieces only at the end." (Woodoku)
- One player on the other direction: "It was suddenly always possible to dig yourself out of a hole… it started to feel a little pointless." (Block Blast)

**Do they quit?** Some say they uninstalled. Many complain and stay: Block Blast still had about 70% five-star reviews in the sample. **Players forgive a loss they caused. They do not forgive a loss handed to them and followed by an ad.**

## What they love
Relaxing ("takes away a lot of stress") · a calm, simple look · satisfying clears and sounds · offline play · no pressure to buy · real challenge · organizing ("made for people that like organize almost everything": the packing theme works).

## What they ask for
One-time "no ads" purchase · switches for animation, vibration, music · dark mode · truly random pieces · real offline play.

## Rules for our game
1. Never an ad while a piece is held.
2. Ads only at natural stops, or when the player taps for one. "No" to a revive means no ad.
3. Always at least one piece that fits, and say so in the store text.
4. Never make it harder because the player is close to a best score.
5. One-time "no ads" on iPhone; rare ads on Android, and say so.
6. Fully offline.
7. Switches for animation, vibration, sound, dark mode from day one.
8. Store pictures show the real game only.

## The gap
Players want a calm, honest block puzzle with fair pieces and ads they can refuse, and no top game gives that. A "load the truck" goal makes a loss feel earned, not rigged.
