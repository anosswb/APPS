## Store rules
- **Apple 4.3 (copycat/spam):** a similar concept can be rejected; Apple's own advice is "a unique app with distinct content and functionality". developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines
- **Google Play ads:** full-screen ads that appear unexpectedly during play are not allowed, also not at the start of a level. Natural breaks (end of a level) are allowed. Rewarded ads the player chooses are fine. support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12271244 (snippet only).

## The scout's three reasons it fails
1. Apple may reject it as one more block puzzle unless the theme and the loop are clearly different.
2. Ads-only on Android pays about $16 per 1,000 videos. The leaders earn through hundreds of millions of installs; a one-person app will not have that.
3. The freight theme has no proven search demand.
