# 04 — Hours clock
**Tool · for Americans paid by the hour · first-guess score 32/40 · about 3 weeks**

## What it is
Your own time clock. Tap when you start, tap when you stop. It keeps your hours, breaks and overtime for each pay period, so you can compare them with your paycheck.

## The special thing
It is YOUR record, not the company's. One tap from the lock screen, and a clean report you can show when the paycheck looks short.

## What to put in version 1
- **One big button:** clock in, clock out, start and end a break.
- **A widget and a lock-screen button** so it takes one tap.
- **This week and this pay period:** regular hours, overtime (after 40 hours a week by default, editable), estimated pay before taxes.
- **Fix a day:** forgot to clock out? Edit the times, with a small "edited" mark.
- **Pay period setup:** weekly, every two weeks, twice a month.
- **Report:** a PDF or text of any period, to save or send.
- A gentle reminder if you are still clocked in after 14 hours.

## What NOT to put
Team features, GPS tracking, tax or legal advice, anything that sounds like "we will get your money back".

## The world (first direction)
A paper time card and a punch clock: card stock, stamped times, a red "IN" and a black "OUT". **Signature:** the punch: a heavy mechanical thunk in sound and in the hand, and the time stamped onto the card.

## Why people come back
Twice a day, every working day. The app becomes more valuable with every week of history.

## How it earns
- **iPhone:** free = the clock and the current week. One-time unlock ($4.99–6.99): full history, reports, more than one job, widgets.
- **Android:** a small ad on the history page; a video to export one report. Never on the clock button.
- **Protected moment:** clocking in and out.

## Build order
1. Time maths as tested functions: shifts over midnight, breaks, weeks, pay periods, overtime, clock changes in spring and fall.
2. Grey box: the button, today, this week.
3. Editing, pay periods, report.
4. Widget and lock-screen button. World and the punch sound, then `/slop-check`.

## Check first
Hours-tracker apps in the US store and their bad reviews (subscriptions, lost data, hard editing are likely) · US overtime basics from the official Department of Labor pages (some states count overtime by the day; the user must be able to set the rule) · US search phrases ("hours tracker", "time card", "work hours", "overtime calculator").

## What Anass does
Says what he would want from this on his own shift. We test with American hourly workers from their online groups.

## Biggest risk
Many hours trackers exist. **Answer:** be the fastest (one tap) and the most trusted (clear report, data that never disappears, pay once). No promise about disputes with an employer.
