# briefs/ — one brief per app idea

Each file is the starting brief for one of the 20 apps in `IDEAS.md`: what it is, what goes in version 1, what stays out, its own world, why people come back, how it earns, the build order, what to check and what Anass does.

**These are first drafts, written 13 Nov 2026 without store research.** They are a good start, not proof. `/score-idea` and `/mine-reviews` come first (steps 2 and 3 of `CHEATSHEET.md`) and may change a brief a lot. Prices, times and "people will like this" are guesses until then.

Rules that are true for every app here, so the briefs do not repeat them:
- No account, no server, no AI bill in version 1. Everything works offline. Data stays on the phone.
- Android earns from ads, iPhone from purchases (Morocco's rails, `playbook/07`). No ad at the moment that is the product.
- Each app has its own world. The "world" section in a brief is ONE first direction; Anass still picks from 3 pictures (`/app-world`). Some first directions here are close to each other (several site apps use yellow). When one of them is built and listed in `WORLDS.md`, the next one must change.
- **For users in the United States, in American English** (dollars, 12-hour clock, feet, pounds, °F). No Arabic, no Darija, nothing built around Morocco (`playbook/09`). Text kept in language files so a second language can come later.
- No medical, money, tax, legal or safety promises.
- Art, sound, fonts, word lists and code: only what we may sell, each one logged with its licence.
- "Build order" always starts with the logic as tested functions and a grey-box version Anass can try.
