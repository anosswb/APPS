# 13 — Pattern studio
**Game · for Americans who colour, quilt or craft to relax · first-guess score 28/40 · about 4 weeks**

## What it is
A calm colouring app built on symmetry. Tap one shape and every matching shape in the pattern fills at once. The patterns are quilt blocks, floor tiles and kaleidoscopes. No score, no wrong answer.

## The special thing
One tap colours the whole pattern. In ten seconds you have made something beautiful, and you can keep it as a wallpaper or see it as a full quilt.

## What to put in version 1
- **30 patterns** drawn by code from geometry: classic quilt-block shapes, tile floors, kaleidoscopes, from simple to rich.
- **8 colour sets,** plus free choice.
- **The symmetry fill** with a soft wave of sound.
- **Repeat view:** your block repeated as a whole quilt or floor.
- Save as a phone wallpaper or share as a picture.
- **A pattern of the day.**

## What NOT to put
Scores, timers, colour-by-number, pictures of animals or people, sewing instructions.

## The world (first direction)
A sewing table: cotton fabric texture, chalk lines, a tin of pins. **Signature:** the fill spreads like patches of cloth being laid down, each with a soft tap.

## Why people come back
The pattern of the day, a gallery of what they made, new patterns each week.

## How it earns
- **Android:** a full-screen ad between patterns (not after the first three); a video to open a special pattern.
- **iPhone:** pattern packs, or everything for one price ($3.99).
- **Protected moment:** while colouring.

## Build order
1. The geometry engine: patterns as shapes with their symmetry groups. Tests that every shape belongs to one group.
2. Grey box: tap, fill, change colour. Is the ten-second magic there?
3. Repeat view, export, pattern of the day.
4. World and sound, then `/slop-check`.

## Check first
Colouring and quilt-design apps in the US store and what their users hate · that every pattern is our own drawing of a traditional, generic shape (no modern designer's pattern) · US search phrases ("coloring", "quilt design", "pattern coloring").

## What Anass does
Picks the colour sets and the world from pictures. We test the first ten seconds on Americans who quilt or colour (they have large online groups).

## Biggest risk
Many colouring apps, and no special advantage for us. **Answer:** the symmetry trick must be felt in the first ten seconds and shown in the first store picture. If testers shrug, stop.
