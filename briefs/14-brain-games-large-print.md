# 14 — Brain games, large print
**Game · for people 55 and older · first-guess score 28/40 · about 5 weeks**

## What it is
Five gentle games in one app: find the pairs, put numbers in order, find words, spot the odd one, continue the pattern. Big text, one task per screen, no rush.

## The special thing
Readable without glasses and never stressful. No timers, no scores that shame, no confusing menus.

## What to put in version 1
- **Five games,** each with levels that grow slowly.
- **"Today's three":** a five-minute set of three games.
- Text at 18 pt or more everywhere, buttons at 56 pt, strong contrast.
- Difficulty that follows the player quietly (`playbook/06`).
- A calendar of days played. A soft chime for success.
- A "how to play" picture before each game, shown again on request.

## What NOT to put
Any claim about memory, ageing or health. Timers by default. Tiny gestures, swipes that are hard to find, "tap anywhere".

## The world (first direction)
Clear and calm: white, deep navy, one warm accent, a very readable typeface. **Signature:** one task in the middle of an uncluttered screen, and a chime where other games throw confetti.

## Why people come back
Today's three is a small daily ritual. The calendar fills. Levels grow without ever feeling hard.

## How it earns
- **Android:** a full-screen ad between games, not inside "Today's three" on the first days; always with a clear close. A video for a hint.
- **iPhone:** one-time unlock of all levels ($3.99–4.99).
- **Protected moment:** inside a game.

## Build order
1. The five games as tested logic with level makers.
2. Grey box of one game at full text size. Test with a person over 60 before building the others.
3. The other four, today's three, calendar.
4. World, then `/slop-check` with the phone's font size at maximum.

## Check first
The big brain-training apps and their reviews (subscriptions and health claims are common: our gap is honest and one-time) · the stores' rules on health claims.

## What Anass does
Finds two or three older family members to test. Watches where they hesitate; that is the bug list.

## Biggest risk
The temptation to promise health benefits, and big competitors. **Answer:** sell calm and clarity, never health.
