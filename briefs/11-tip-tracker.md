# 11 — Tip tracker
**Tool · for American servers, bartenders, baristas, drivers and stylists · first-guess score 30/40 · about 2 weeks**

## What it is
Log your tips after each shift in ten seconds. See what you really make per hour, which days and shifts pay best, and your totals for the week, month and year.

## The special thing
Ten seconds at the end of a shift, with one hand. And it answers the question every tipped worker has: "Is Friday night really worth it?"

## What to put in version 1
- **Quick entry:** cash tips, card tips, hours, tip-out to others. Big number keys.
- **Hourly truth:** tips plus base wage, per hour.
- **Best days and shifts** as a simple chart.
- **Totals** by week, month and year. A goal for the week.
- **Export** the year as a file (useful at tax time; we give the numbers, never advice).
- More than one job.

## What NOT to put
Tax advice, bank links, anything shared with an employer.

## The world (first direction)
A diner guest check and a tip jar: green-lined paper, handwriting-style numbers, coins. **Signature:** the entry drops into a jar that fills through the week, with the sound of coins and bills.

## Why people come back
Every shift. The jar and the weekly goal, and the picture of their real income getting clearer.

## How it earns
- **iPhone:** free = entry and this week. One-time unlock ($3.99–4.99): full history, charts, export, more jobs.
- **Android:** a small ad on the totals page; a video to export. Never on entry.
- **Protected moment:** entering tips.

## Build order
1. The numbers as tested functions (hourly rate, tip-out, periods).
2. Grey box: can a shift be entered in ten seconds?
3. Charts, goal, export.
4. World and sound, then `/slop-check`.

## Check first
Tip trackers in the US store and their reviews · the words tipped workers use (Reddit groups for servers and bartenders) · US search phrases ("tip tracker", "tip log", "server tips").

## What Anass does
Picks the world. We test with American servers and bartenders from their online groups.

## Biggest risk
Small and simple, so easy to copy, and several exist. **Answer:** two weeks of work, a better feel than the others, and it can bring users to Hours clock and the Shift app.
