# 16 — Knots
**Tool · for Americans who fish, camp, boat, or tie down a load · first-guess score 28/40 · about 5 weeks**

## What it is
A pocket guide to the 40 knots people really use, each shown step by step with a clear moving drawing you can pause, slow down and turn around.

## The special thing
You find the knot by what you need to DO ("tie a hook", "tie down a load", "join two ropes"), not by its name. And the drawing is clear enough to follow with the rope in your hands.

## What to put in version 1
- **Find by job:** fishing, camping, boating, hauling and tie-downs, around the house.
- **40 knots,** each with a step-by-step animation: pause, slow, step back, mirror for left-handed people.
- One line on when to use it and when not to.
- Favourites. Works with no signal, in the woods or on the water.
- **Knot of the week.**

## What NOT to put
Climbing and rescue knots (lives depend on them; too risky), long history texts, videos that need internet.

## The world (first direction)
A field guide: canvas, brass eyelets, hand-drawn rope in two colours. **Signature:** the rope draws itself along its path, so the eye can follow one strand.

## Why people come back
Before a trip and at the moment of need. Not daily, so it is a buy-once app.

## How it earns
- **iPhone:** 8 knots free, one-time unlock for all ($3.99–4.99).
- **Android:** a small ad on the list page; a video to open one knot for the day.
- **Protected moment:** while following the steps.

## Build order
1. One knot as a rope path drawn by code and animated. If this looks clear, the app works; if not, stop.
2. The player: pause, slow, step, mirror.
3. The other 39 paths (the main cost), the find-by-job lists.
4. World, then `/slop-check` outdoors in sunlight.

## Check first
Knot apps in the US store, their prices and reviews · that every drawing is our own · US search phrases ("fishing knots", "knots guide", "how to tie").

## What Anass does
Picks the world. Tests whether he can tie a knot he does not know from the first animation.

## Biggest risk
Forty clear animations are a lot of work, and good knot apps exist. **Answer:** start with 10 fishing and tie-down knots, launch small, add the rest if people come.
