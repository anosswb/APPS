# 10 — Day sleep
**Tool · for night workers · first-guess score 30/40 · about 3 weeks**

## What it is
Help to fall asleep in the morning after a night shift. You say when you finish; it gives a simple plan and a wind-down screen made for daylight.

## The special thing
Built for sleeping at 8 am, not 11 pm. It also solves a real problem: the people at home who wake you.

## What to put in version 1
- **A plan from your finish time:** last coffee, wind-down start, sleep window, wake time.
- **Wind-down screen:** dim amber, slow breathing guide, steady sound (brown noise, fan, rain).
- **"Do not wake me" card:** a picture to send to the family ("Sleeping until 15:00. Night shift.").
- An alarm for the wake time.
- Sounds keep playing with the screen off.

## What NOT to put
Sleep tracking, sleep scores, promises of better sleep or health.

## The world (first direction)
Dark only. Warm amber light, no blue, everything fades and nothing slides. **Signature:** the screen dims like a lamp turned down by hand.

## Why people come back
It is used after every night shift. It remembers your times.

## How it earns
- **iPhone:** one-time unlock ($3.99): all sounds, plans for rotating shifts.
- **Android:** almost nothing. An ad cannot be shown to someone falling asleep.
- **Protected moment:** the whole wind-down.

## Build order
1. Plan maths from the finish time, with tests.
2. Grey box of the wind-down with one sound. Does audio keep playing with the screen off on both phones?
3. The card to share, the alarm.
4. World, then `/slop-check` in a dark room.

## Check first
Sleep apps for shift workers and their reviews · sounds with a licence that allows selling (CC0) · the stores' health-claim rules.

## What Anass does
Tries the wind-down after a real night shift. Says what truly keeps him awake in the morning.

## Biggest risk
It earns only on iPhone and sleep apps are many. **Answer:** this may be better as a part of the Shift app (#1) than as its own app. Decide after the Shift app has users.
