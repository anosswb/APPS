# 15 — Invoice, pay once
**Tool · for American one-person trades: handymen, cleaners, landscapers, painters · first-guess score 28/40 · about 4 weeks**

## What it is
Make an estimate or an invoice on the phone in two minutes, right at the customer's door, and send it as a clean PDF. Everything stays on the phone.

## The special thing
You pay once. Most invoice apps are subscriptions, and people who send five invoices a month hate paying every month.

## What to put in version 1
- **New estimate or invoice:** customer, lines (work and materials), quantity, price, tax rate you set, total.
- **Turn an estimate into an invoice** in one tap.
- **Your saved items** ("Lawn mow, front and back, $45").
- **PDF** with your name, logo and payment note. Send by text or email.
- **Mark as paid.** See what is still owed.
- A customer list. Photos attached to a job.

## What NOT to put
Card payments, bank links, accounting, tax advice, accounts or cloud.

## The world (first direction)
A carbon-copy invoice book: off-white paper, blue ruled lines, a pressed-ink look. **Signature:** the finished invoice tears off the pad with a paper rip.

## Why people come back
Every job needs one. The saved items and customers make each new invoice faster than the last.

## How it earns
- **iPhone:** free = 3 invoices a month. One-time unlock ($14.99–19.99; it replaces a monthly fee, so people accept more): unlimited, logo, saved items, photos.
- **Android:** a small ad on the list page; a video to send one more invoice this month.
- **Protected moment:** writing and sending an invoice.

## Build order
1. Invoice maths and the PDF maker, with tests (rounding, tax, numbering).
2. Grey box: one invoice from nothing to sent in two minutes.
3. Estimates, saved items, customers, paid/unpaid.
4. World, then `/slop-check`.

## Check first
Invoice apps in the US store, their monthly prices and their bad reviews (expect "too expensive", "forced subscription") · what small US trades put on an invoice · US search phrases ("invoice maker", "estimate maker", "contractor invoice").

## What Anass does
Picks the world. We test with American handymen and cleaners from their online groups.

## Biggest risk
Big companies own this search, and it earns only on iPhone. **Answer:** "pay once" in the title and the first screenshot; a narrow first audience (one-person trades).
