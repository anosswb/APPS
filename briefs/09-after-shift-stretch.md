# 09 — After-shift stretch
**Tool · for people who stand, lift or drive all day · first-guess score 30/40 · about 5 weeks**

## What it is
Five-minute stretch routines for after a shift. Pick what is tired (back, feet and calves, neck and shoulders, hands) and follow simple drawings with a timer.

## The special thing
Made for work bodies, not gym bodies. You can do it in work clothes, in a locker room or next to the car, with no mat.

## What to put in version 1
- **Pick an area** or "everything, 5 minutes".
- **About 40 stretches,** each with one clear drawing, a name and 30–45 seconds.
- A soft sound at each change, so you do not need to look.
- A simple log: days done this week.
- A reminder at the end of the shift.
- A clear line: "Not medical advice. Stop if it hurts."

## What NOT to put
Words like pain relief, treat, heal, injury. Workout plans, calories, body photos.

## The world (first direction)
A locker room: work-jacket navy, chalk-white line drawings. **Signature:** one figure drawn with a single line that moves smoothly from pose to pose.

## Why people come back
It is tied to something that happens anyway (the end of the shift), it is five minutes, and the week view only counts up.

## How it earns
- **iPhone:** free = the 5-minute "everything" routine. One-time unlock ($4.99): all areas and your own routines.
- **Android:** a video to open one area for the day. No ad during a routine.
- **Protected moment:** the routine.

## Build order
1. The list of stretches, checked against trusted public guidance (written in our own words).
2. Grey box: one routine with placeholder drawings and the timer sounds.
3. The drawings in one style. This is the main cost.
4. Areas, log, reminder. World, then `/slop-check`.

## Check first
Stretching apps and their reviews (subscriptions are a likely complaint) · the health rules of both stores (`playbook/08`) · how to get 40 drawings in one style with rights we own.

## What Anass does
Says which parts hurt after a real shift and which stretches he would really do in work clothes. Tests the first routine after a shift.

## Biggest risk
The drawings cost time, and health wording is strict. **Answer:** start with 12 stretches and one routine; never promise a health result.
