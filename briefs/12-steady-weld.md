# 12 — Steady weld
**Game · for one-thumb arcade players and people in the trades · first-guess score 30/40 · about 3 weeks**

## What it is
Hold your finger down and follow the seam to weld two pieces of metal together. Too fast and the weld is thin, too slow and you burn through, off the line and it is ugly. At the end you see your bead and get a grade.

## The special thing
A steady-hand game that looks and sounds like real welding: the arc, the sparks, the glowing bead cooling from orange to grey. Welders will show it to each other.

## What to put in version 1
- **One-finger play:** speed and steadiness along a line.
- **Joints that get harder:** straight, corner, curve, pipe, overhead (the line shakes more).
- **Your bead, close up,** with a grade from the shop foreman.
- **A daily job,** the same for everyone, with a picture to share.
- Helmets and gloves to unlock.
- The screen darkens like a welding helmet when the arc starts.

## What NOT to put
Real welding instruction or safety advice, a long career mode.

## The world (first direction)
A dark workshop: black steel, one blue-white arc, orange heat. **Signature:** the helmet-dark flash and the bead that cools while you watch.

## Why people come back
"I can do a cleaner one." The daily job and sharing the bead picture. Honest: arcade games have a short life.

## How it earns
- **Android:** a video to retry a joint without losing the streak; a full-screen ad every third job, not in the first session.
- **iPhone:** one-time unlock of all joints and gear ($1.99–2.99).
- **Protected moment:** while the arc is on.

## Build order
1. Grey box: the line, the finger, the scoring of speed and steadiness. One full day on the feel.
2. The bead drawing from the player's real path. The grade.
3. Joints, daily job, share picture, unlocks.
4. World, sound (a real arc sound with a licence we can use), haptics, then `/slop-check`.

## Check first
Welding and steady-hand games in the US store · where American welders talk online, for testers and for sharing.

## What Anass does
Says if it looks and sounds true (he has seen real welding). Plays the grey box.

## Biggest risk
Played for a few days. **Answer:** three weeks of work, a strong first ten seconds, and a share picture that brings new players for free.
