# 19 — CDL practice
**Study app · for Americans preparing for the commercial driver's license written tests · first-guess score 27/40 · about 6 weeks, most of it writing questions**

## What it is
Practice questions for the written tests that truck and bus drivers must pass: general knowledge, air brakes, combination vehicles and the main endorsements. Short rounds, clear explanations.

## The special thing
It explains WHY an answer is right in one plain sentence, and it keeps bringing back the questions you got wrong until you know them. Made for people who have not studied in years.

## What to put in version 1
- **Rounds of 10 questions** by topic.
- **A full practice test** with the same number of questions and pass mark as the real one.
- **Weak spots:** wrong answers come back sooner.
- A "ready" meter per topic.
- About 600 questions written by us from the official manual.
- A clear line: "Practice only. Always study your state's official manual."

## What NOT to put
A promise to pass, copied questions from any site or app, state-by-state claims we have not checked.

## The world (first direction)
The cab of a truck at dawn: dashboard gauges, highway green and white. **Signature:** the "ready" meter is a dashboard gauge that climbs into the green.

## Why people come back
A test date is coming. Daily rounds, the gauge climbing, weak spots shrinking. After they pass, they leave: this is a few weeks of heavy use per person, with new people all the time.

## How it earns
- **Android:** a full-screen ad after a round; a video for a second try on a test.
- **iPhone:** general knowledge free, one-time unlock for all topics and full tests ($6.99–9.99).
- **Protected moment:** during a round or a test.

## Build order
1. A test of 60 questions in a grey box. How long does one good, checked question take?
2. The question pipeline: Claude drafts from the official manual, every answer is checked against the manual page, wording is our own.
3. Weak-spot timing, full test, gauges.
4. World, then `/slop-check`.

## Check first
The official CDL manual and how tests are structured (number of questions, pass mark; this can differ by state) · CDL practice apps in the US store and their reviews (wrong answers, too many ads are likely) · that government manuals may be used as a source of facts while our questions stay our own words · US search phrases ("cdl practice test", "cdl prep").

## What Anass does
Decides if he accepts a content-heavy app. Picks the world.

## Biggest risk
One wrong answer hurts someone's test and our rating, and the category is crowded. **Answer:** only continue if the 60-question test shows we can be accurate and clearer than the others.
