# 08 — Tunnel dig puzzle
**Game · for casual puzzle players · first-guess score 30/40 · about 5 weeks**

## What it is
A puzzle seen as a cut through the ground. Drag to dig tunnels that join each entrance to its exit. Tunnels may not cross, and each deeper layer of earth adds a new rule.

## The special thing
You dig. Every chapter is a deeper layer with something new: rock you must go around, water, soft soil, a machine that only goes straight.

## What to put in version 1
- **Levels made by code,** each with one answer.
- **Six layers,** about 40 levels each, each layer with one new rule.
- Hints that show one section. Undo. No timer.
- A map of the dig going down, so the player sees what is ahead.
- **A daily dig.**

## What NOT to put
A move limit that forces replays, lives, a timer.

## The world (first direction)
A geology poster: bands of warm earth colours, helmet-lamp light. **Signature:** when a tunnel connects, lamps switch on along it one by one and the phone gives a low rumble.

## Why people come back
The next layer and its new rule, the map going deeper, the daily dig. Difficulty in waves, with a hard "rock" level at the end of each block of five.

## How it earns
- **Android:** a video for a hint; a full-screen ad every third level, not in the first ten minutes.
- **iPhone:** layers 1–2 free, one-time unlock for the rest ($3.99).
- **Protected moment:** while digging.

## Build order
1. Level maker and solver with a one-answer test. Add rules one at a time.
2. Grey box: is dragging a tunnel pleasant? Does rule 2 feel new?
3. Layers, map, hints, daily.
4. World and sound, then `/slop-check`.

## Check first
Path-joining puzzles in the stores (there are many) and their reviews · that our rules and look are clearly our own.

## What Anass does
Plays the grey box. If he knows tunnelling, gives real words and details for the layers.

## Biggest risk
Path puzzles are common. **Answer:** the new rule in every layer and the dig world. If the grey box feels like every other one, stop.
