# 17 — Site calculator
**Tool · for American builders, framers, concrete and site workers · first-guess score 28/40 · about 2 weeks**

## What it is
The few calculations done every day on a site, each on one clear screen: concrete volume, area, slope, unit changes, steel weight, stairs, how many blocks or tiles.

## The special thing
It thinks in feet, inches and fractions the way American trades do (5' 7 3/8" + 2' 9 1/2"), with huge keys for gloves, and it keeps the last 20 results like a tape.

## What to put in version 1
- **Feet-inch-fraction maths:** add, subtract, multiply, divide, to the nearest 1/16.
- **Concrete:** slab, footing, column, in cubic yards and bags; with a waste percentage.
- **Area and volume** of simple shapes.
- **Slope:** percent, ratio, angle, in both directions.
- **Units:** feet and inches to metric and back. **Roof pitch** (rise over 12).
- **Counts:** blocks, tiles, with waste.
- **Stairs:** rise and run.
- **The tape:** the last 20 results, with a name for each, to share as text.

## What NOT to put
Prices, structural or safety calculations, anything that needs an engineer's stamp.

## The world (first direction)
A steel tape measure: yellow, black marks, strong numbers. **Signature:** each result rolls out on a measuring tape.

## Why people come back
It is on the phone when the question comes up, and the tape remembers.

## How it earns
- **iPhone:** one-time unlock ($3.99): all calculators and the tape.
- **Android:** a small ad on the tape page only, away from the keys.
- **Protected moment:** typing a calculation.

## Build order
1. Every formula as a tested function, with units handled carefully.
2. Grey box of the concrete screen. Test with gloves.
3. The other screens, the tape.
4. World, then `/slop-check` in sunlight.

## Check first
Construction calculator apps in the US store, their prices (some are expensive) and reviews · which calculations American tradespeople say they do most (trade forums, Reddit).

## What Anass does
Lists the calculations he sees people do by hand on site. Tests the grey box with gloves. Americans check the fraction input.

## Biggest risk
Not opened every day, and many calculators exist. **Answer:** two weeks of work only. It can also bring site workers to the Shift app and the daily checks.
