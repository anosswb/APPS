# IDEAS.md — 20 apps to choose from

Written 13 Nov 2026. **Market rule (Anass): United States first, in English. No Arabic, no Darija, no app built around Morocco.**

**Every score is a first guess, made without store research.** Pick the ones you like, then `/score-idea <number>` replaces the guess with proof (real US searches, earning competitors, review quotes) before anything is built. Scoring rules: `playbook/02`. Each app has its own world; none shares a look with Gobblo or with another app on this list.

**A full brief for each app is in `briefs/`** (same numbers): what it is, what goes in version 1, what stays out, the look, why people come back, how it earns, the build order and what Anass does.

## My pick (three apps, three different ways to earn)
1. **Daily paper puzzles (#2)** — the ads game. It fits the only Android rail we have, American ad prices are high, and the levels are made by code.
2. **Shift app (#1)** — the iPhone tool. You know the 12-hour shift from the inside, and millions of Americans work them (nurses alone are a huge group).
3. **Hours clock (#4)** or **Operator daily checks (#6)** — the work tool that people pay for once.
A small quick one between big ones: **Tip tracker (#11)** (about two weeks) or **Steady weld (#12)** (about three).
Gobblo ships first: it is the closest to money.

## How to choose
Tell Claude the numbers you like (for example "2, 1 and 4"). It runs `/score-idea` on each and comes back with proof and a yes or no. Then `/make-app <name>` starts the path in `CHEATSHEET.md`.

## How to add an idea
Copy `templates/idea-card.md`, fill it, score it, add one row to the list. It must be for US users. Ideas with proof replace guesses. Dead ideas move to the parked table with one line of why.
