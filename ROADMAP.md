# ROADMAP — from today to app income

Written 13 Nov 2026. Dates move; the order and the gates do not. No date here is a promise of money.

## The strategy in five lines
1. **A portfolio, not one bet.** Several small, well-made apps. Most earn little. One or two earn most of the money. We find them by shipping and measuring.
2. **United States first, in English.** American users are worth the most in ads and in purchases. Three ways to earn with Morocco's payment rails: an ads game on Android, a paid tool on iPhone for shift workers, a paid work tool on iPhone for trades.
3. **Every app has its own world and one special thing.** Same method, never the same look.
4. **Measure, then grow, fix or park** after 30 days. No feelings.
5. **Anass's hours are protected.** Claude does the work; Anass chooses, pays and tests.

## Phase 1 — December (accounts open)
Day 1 list, in this order:
1. Google Play Console: create the account, finish identity verification, create the Gobblo app, **start the closed test the same day** with the whole tester group (the 14-day clock decides the launch date).
2. Apple Developer: enrol, sign the paid apps agreement, fill bank and tax forms (check that the Moroccan bank is accepted), **join the Small Business Program**.
3. AdMob and RevenueCat: link the apps. Test IDs stay in every build except the store build.

Then:
- Gobblo: TestFlight to 5–10 iPhone users; 2–3 updates during the Android test; request production after day 14; submit to Apple.
- App two (the first picked idea, if proof holds): world chosen by Anass from 3 pictures in week 1; built in weeks 2–4; **its own closed test starts as soon as it runs**, also when unfinished.

**Gate:** Gobblo submitted to both stores. App two in closed test.

## Phase 2 — January
- Gobblo live. Read the numbers weekly (playbook/06 A7 and B). Fix the first session. Answer every review.
- App two: soft launch in Canada or Australia, then the United States.

**Gate (end of January):** Gobblo has 30 days of real numbers → grow, fix once, or park (playbook/07).

## Phase 3 — February and March
- App two: 30-day verdict. For a winner: more content, US videos, and only then a second language (Spanish first, playbook/09).
- App three: test and launch. If it is a tool, iPhone first (it earns there), Android after.
- App four (the third picked idea): proof and world.
- Short videos for the best app every week.

## Phase 4 — April onward: the rhythm
Each month, one of two things, chosen with real numbers in `tools/roi.py`:
- **Grow the best app** (content, US videos, a second platform, later a second language), when an extra month there is worth more than a new app; or
- **Ship a new small app** (4–6 weeks) from the top of IDEAS.md.
Every quarter: re-read `research/`, re-check the Morocco rails, re-check both stores' rules, update prices.

## Money milestones (no dates on purpose)
| Milestone | What it proves |
|---|---|
| First $1 from a stranger | The whole machine works: store, payment, payout. |
| $100 in a month | One app has real users who stay. |
| $500 in a month | A growth path exists. Put hours into that app. |
| $1,000 in a month, three months in a row | Time to talk about reducing shifts. |

## About leaving the job
This is Anass's decision. A safe rule to consider, not an order:
- App income **after all costs and taxes** at or above the salary for **3 months in a row**, from **more than one app** (or more than one store), **and** 6 months of living costs saved.
- Before that, a middle step if the employer allows: fewer shifts, not zero.
One app on one store can lose its income in a day (a policy change, a suspension, a copy). Two legs before standing on them.

## What would change this plan
Google opens merchant accounts for Morocco (then Android can sell too) · one app clearly wins early (then stop new apps and grow it) · a store rejects our kind of app (read the rule, fix the cause, write it in playbook/13).
