# Builds — how to get the Shift app on a real Android phone

Built by GitHub, for free, with no Expo account. A real installed app, signed for testing only (it can never go to Google Play).

## Get the test app (about 15–20 minutes, most of it waiting)
1. Open the repo on github.com and tap **Actions**.
2. On the left, tap **Shift app test build (Android APK)**.
3. If a run with a green tick is already there, tap it. If not: tap **Run workflow**, keep `main`, tap the green **Run workflow**, and wait for the green tick.
4. In the run, scroll down to **Artifacts** and tap **shift-app-test-apk**. A zip downloads (you must be logged in to GitHub).
5. On the phone: open the zip, tap the `.apk`, allow "install unknown apps" when Android asks, tap **Install**.

If the run shows a red cross: tap it, tap the red step, copy the last 30 lines and send them to Claude.

## What to try (10 minutes)
1. **Pay:** type your hourly rate and your numbers with the keyboard. Tap **Clock in now**, or the pencil to type the time you started. Tap a past day to type its In and Out times, and the Holiday switch.
2. **Extra pay:** set Night hours extra (and the night start and end), Saturdays, Sundays, holidays.
3. **Pay stub check:** pick the period with the arrows, type the hours and gross pay from your stub, read the line under it.
4. **Notes:** on Today tap **Turn on** (or My day → Notes on work days). Allow notifications. On your next work day you get a lunch note in the middle of the shift and one 2 hours before clock-out.
5. **Widget:** press and hold your home screen → Widgets → Shift app → drag **Next rest** onto the screen.
6. **Look:** My day → Look → Light, then Dark.

## What this build is not
No backup yet. No pay period or payday yet (week and month only). No iPhone widget.
