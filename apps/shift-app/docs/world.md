## The rule he gave after seeing the first pictures
One job per tab, few colours, few words: "so much colour and text and things at one tab feel distracting… put each thing in a place." Tabs: Today · Calendar · My day · Pay.

## Rejected directions (and why)
- **Amber and the sunset sky, boxes for days** (passes one and two): on his phone he called the boxes ugly and the colours childish. He sent reference screens: calm blue, black and green, soft glass, a week strip with dots. We took the patterns, not the screens.
- **B, The Board** (signal board, condensed capitals): he did not pick it.
- **C, The Dial as the main screen** (light): he loved the ring but not as the first screen; the ring moved to My day, in the dark look.
- Fonts 2, 3, 4 (Anybody, Syne, Big Shoulders): he chose font 1.
