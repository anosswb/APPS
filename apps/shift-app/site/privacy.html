Privacy Policy

Shift app · Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLISHING]

The short version

You do not make an account. Your shifts, rest days, clock-in times and pay numbers stay on your phone. We do not have a server, and we never see your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your calendar (work days, rest days), your shift times and your day plan.
  • Your clock-in and clock-out log, your hourly rate and your extra-pay numbers.
  • Your answers to the first-open questions (type of work, shift length).

This data is stored only on your device. If you delete the app, it is deleted.

Backups

If you tap "Save a backup", the app makes a file and opens your phone's share screen. You choose where it goes (for example Google Drive or your email). That place's own privacy policy then applies. We never receive the file.

Notifications

The lunch note and the "2 hours to go" note are made on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone settings.

Ads (Android only)

On Android, the free app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address) and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. The iPhone version has no ads.

Purchases (iPhone only)

Shift app Plus is sold through Apple. Apple handles the payment. We do not see your card or your Apple ID.

Children

Shift app is made for working adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
