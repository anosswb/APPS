# Brief — Paper Pines

Sources: `research/ideas/daily-paper-puzzles-market.md` and `research/ideas/daily-paper-puzzles-reviews.md` at the repo root (13 Nov 2026). The store pages themselves could not be opened from the cloud session, so store numbers come from search snippets and a scraper tool; treat them as rough.

## The person
An American adult, 30 and up, who likes a pencil puzzle with coffee or in bed. Plays 5–15 minutes, often daily, one hand. Has tried the big free puzzle apps and is tired of them.

## What they hate in other apps (565 real reviews read)
| # | Complaint | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Too many ads, and ads in the middle of a move | about 56 of 73 low-star reviews of the biggest nonogram app; about 88 of 103 for the biggest sudoku app (taken during an angry spike) |
| 2 | Coins or packs to unlock more puzzles; "let me pay once" | 24 iOS + about 12 Play reviews of the main Tents app; 12 say they would pay once |
| 3 | Updates that break things or lose progress | about 19 reviews |
| 4 | Pop-ups, events, "Great move" animations | 13 reviews |
| 5 | Lives, game over, forced auto-correct | 10 reviews |
Also asked for: dark mode (19), a one-time purchase (about 15), hints that explain the next step (8), bigger tap targets or zoom (about 15).
What they love and we must keep: calm play with no pop-ups, hints that do not punish, offline play, a free daily puzzle.

## The gap
They all wrap the puzzle in ads, coins or subscriptions, and nobody offers a calm page you pay for once, where nothing interrupts a move and the hint explains the thinking.

## Competitors (rough)
Small makers own the Tents niche (the main one has a few hundred iOS ratings; three new Tents apps with a daily puzzle appeared in 2026). Bigger daily-puzzle collections use ads plus subscriptions or packs. So: real demand, modest size, weak looks. The risk is that the niche is small; the answer is more puzzle types on the same "page" later.

## Version 1 does
1. One daily puzzle, the same for everyone, small on Monday and big on Sunday.
2. The Book: an endless row of numbered puzzles that grow in waves.
3. Hints that find a mistake first, then explain the next step in a sentence.
Plus: undo, drag to mark, light and dark, progress saved on the phone, a rules page.

## Version 1 does NOT do (and why)
- Other puzzle types (one type done well first).
- Accounts, sync, leaderboards (no server).
- Timers on screen, lives, streak pressure (the reviews).
- Sizes above 10 × 10 (squares would be too small to tap).

## The one number
Puzzles finished on day 1. Target: 4 or more. Then day-1, day-7 and day-30 return (playbook/06).

## Five rules from the review research
1. Never interrupt a move or a puzzle. Android ads only between puzzles, with a visible close.
2. One price unlocks everything on iPhone. No coins. "No ads" must be literally true. Dailies free.
3. Free hints that name the reason. No lives. Timer and stats optional, off by default.
4. Dark mode, big squares, drag to fill, undo, offline, no repeated puzzles.
5. Protect progress in every update. Art must not look AI-made.
