## Description (both stores)
Paper Pines is a puzzle page for people who like to think in peace.

Pitch one tent beside every tree. Tents never touch. The numbers tell you how many go in each row and column. That is all there is to learn, and there is always exactly one answer.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
• Never a guess. Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone. We test this on every puzzle we make.
• Hints that teach. Stuck? A hint shows the next step and tells you why it is true.
• Nothing interrupts a puzzle. No pop-ups, no timers, no lives.
• One puzzle for everyone, every day. Small on Monday, big on Sunday.
• A whole book of puzzles that grow as you do, from 5 × 5 to 10 × 10.
• Looks like the Sunday paper. Day and night versions.
• Works offline. No account.

FAIR AND SIMPLE
The daily puzzle, the last seven days, all hints and the first part of the book are free.
[iPhone] One purchase unlocks everything else. No subscription, and no ads at all.
[Android] Free with a few ads between puzzles, never during one.
