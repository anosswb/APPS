# Brief — Dinkline (working title)

## The person
The volunteer who runs drop-in open play at a US park or club: 40–70, 8–24 players, 1–4 courts, outdoors, a paddle in one hand. Today they use a paddle rack, a whiteboard or memory. (Evidence: research/2026-11-21-pickleball-reviews.md. Real-court pain comes from blogs and search snippets, not from app reviews: no queue app has reviews yet.)

## The pain (labelled)
- "Am I next?" asked again and again; arguments about who sat out twice (source says, blogs/snippets).
- The same foursome keeps playing together (source says).
- Score keeper apps lose the score, get side-out server numbers wrong, or charge a big fee (seen in reviews of scorers).

## What v1 does (3 things)
1. **The line:** add names in seconds (Return adds the next one), late arrivals at the end, sit out / back in, leave after this game, move up, Undo for any mistake.
2. **Fair rotation onto 1–8 courts:** next four go on automatically; all-four-off (paddle rack) or winners-stay (max 2 wins); partners chosen so the same pairs don't repeat.
3. **Big scorer:** side-out (with the 0-0-2 start and server 1/2) or rally, game to 11/15/21, win by 2, game point, switch-ends reminder, Undo; the result feeds the rotation.
All on one phone, saved on the phone, no account, screen stays on.

## What v1 does NOT do
No accounts, no sharing to other phones, no TV/big-board mode, no singles rotation, no skill-level courts, no timed rotation, no stats history between sessions, no tournaments or ladders, no ads yet, no purchase yet.

## The one number
Sessions per device per week in the first month (a session = 8+ games ended). Target: groups come back weekly (week-2 return > 30%, guess).
