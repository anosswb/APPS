# Dinkline logo: prompts for an image AI (22 Nov 2026)

For: the app icon (1024 × 1024) and the splash. Works in ChatGPT image, Google Flow (Nano Banana) or Midjourney.

**Who plays pickleball in the US:** many are 35–65, with money to spend, who play at clubs and country clubs, plus a fast-growing younger crowd. "Premium" for them means calm, sharp and expensive-looking, like a watch or golf brand, not a cartoon.

**Rules for every prompt**
- No letters in the icon. AI often spells words wrong, and app icons are too small for words anyway. The name goes under the icon on the phone.
- Make 4 versions of each prompt. Pick the one that still reads at thumb size (look at it at 60 px).
- Never ask for "like Selkirk / JOOLA / PPA / Nike". Our own mark only.
- Send me the picture you like and I'll cut it into the Android and iPhone icon files and check it at small size.
