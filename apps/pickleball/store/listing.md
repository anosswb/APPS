- iPhone: Queue, courts and score
- Android: Run pickleball open play from one phone: who's next, fair rotation, big score.

## Description
Running open play shouldn't mean a whiteboard, a pile of paddles and "am I next?" every two minutes.

Dinkline runs the line for you on one phone:
- Add players as they arrive. Type a name, tap Return, next name.
- When a court opens, the next four go on. Partners are mixed so the same pairs don't keep playing together.
- Pick how people come off: all four off, winners stay, or winners split.
- Keep score with big numbers you can read in the sun. Side-out or rally scoring, game to 11, 15 or 21. The app calls the score ("4-2-1") and tracks who serves.
- Show the board: hold the phone up and everyone sees who's next.
- Someone needs a break? Sit them out. They're back in line with one tap.
- Made a mistake? Undo.

No account. No sign-in. Everything stays on your phone. The screen stays on while you play.
